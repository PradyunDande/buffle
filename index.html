THE RULES OF THE REALM

How to Play

  1. Pick a mode. Story Mode is a solo campaign of four chapters. Co-op Boss puts two players in one camera frame against any boss. 1v1 Duel pits you against a friend.
  2. Move to attack. Every rep deals damage: clean full-depth reps deal 2, half reps 1, sloppy form 0.5. A held plank deals damage every 1.2 seconds.
  3. Survive the phases. Bosses switch exercises as their health drops — squats, jumping jacks, then planks. Play pauses briefly so you can change position.
  4. Parry attacks. When the boss winds up, land a rep within 2.5 seconds to parry for +2 damage. Miss it and lose a heart. You have three hearts per fight.
  5. Sync in co-op. Finish within 700ms of your teammate for +3 SYNC damage and a combo multiplier. Every five syncs triggers FRENZY: double damage for five seconds.
  6. Party up online. Remote Party lets up to six people on their own devices fight a boss together. Share the party code, and everyone's camera appears in a grid. Boss health is multiplied by the number of players.
  7. Win the duel. Clean-rep streaks multiply your points. Pull the rope 25 points your way for a knockout, or lead when the 45 seconds run out.

No camera? Use 1 and 2 for clean test reps; Q and W add half reps.
