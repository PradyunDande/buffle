How to Play

  1. Pick a mode. Boss Scuffle and 1v1 Scuffle each start by asking whether you're playing locally, sharing one camera, or online, each on your own device. Local Boss Scuffle asks how many people are playing (one, or two side by side in one camera) and how hard to go, then sends a random fairy-tale monster. Easy sticks to standing moves, Normal adds push-ups, and Hard adds planks and a faster pace. 1v1 Duel pits you against a friend.
  2. Move to attack. Every rep deals damage: clean full-depth reps deal 2, half reps 1, sloppy form 0.5. Push-ups deal 1.5× and punches half as much, since they come fast. A held plank deals damage every 1.2 seconds.
  3. Stay in frame. Squats, jumping jacks, and punches only need your upper body in view. Push-ups and planks need a side view.
  4. Follow the boss's calls. Every so often the boss calls a new move for each player at random: squats, punches, jumping jacks, push-ups, or planks. The monster shouts each call at you by name, and teammates can be on different moves. Your reps pause for a few seconds while you switch; if everyone is switching, the clock and the boss wait too.
  5. Parry attacks. When the boss winds up, land a rep within 2.5 seconds to parry for +2 damage. Miss it and lose a heart. You have three hearts per fight.
  6. Sync in co-op. Finish within 700ms of your teammate for +3 SYNC damage and a combo multiplier. Every five syncs triggers FRENZY: double damage for five seconds.
  7. Party up online. Online Boss Scuffle lets up to six people on their own devices fight a boss together. Share the party code, and everyone's camera appears in a grid. Online play has voice chat: Mute in the header silences your mic, and each other player's camera has its own Mute and volume slider. Boss health is multiplied by the number of players.
  8. Win the 1v1. Pick a difficulty and the game calls random moves for both players at once, so you always race on the same exercise. Clean-rep streaks multiply your points. Pull the rope 25 points your way for a knockout, or lead when the 45 seconds run out. Find Online Match pairs you with a stranger on their own device, always on Hard.

No camera? Use 1 and 2 for clean test reps; Q and W add half reps.
