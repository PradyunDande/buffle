THE RULES OF THE COURT

How to Play

  1. Stand together. Put two people side by side in one camera frame. Solo Practice needs one.
  2. Choose a trial. Squats, jumping jacks, or experimental push-ups. Full movements score best.
  3. Sync your reps. Finish within 700ms of your teammate for a +3 point SYNC bonus. Build a combo to multiply points.
  4. Trigger FRENZY. Every five syncs gives your team five seconds of double points.
  5. Beat the target. You have 45 seconds. Reach the team target to win.

No camera? Use 1 and 2 for clean test reps; Q and W add half reps.
